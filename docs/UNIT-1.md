# Unit 1 — complete authoring inventory

Generated from the playable curriculum using `pnpm curriculum:plan`. The former eight-unit outline remains in `docs/CURRICULUM.md` as historical planning documentation; it is not loaded into the app.

Fourteen lessons, estimated 3–5 minutes each. Romanization belongs in teaching/support and explicit sound-label typing. Audio uses full syllables. Guided expressions disclose unfamiliar letters and register.

## 1. Meet Hangul [u1-day-01]

**Objective:** Recognize ㅏ, ㅗ and ㅣ and recall their sound labels.
**Prerequisites:** None
**New:** ㅏ [hangul-314f]; ㅗ [hangul-3157]; ㅣ [hangul-3163]
**Review:** First lesson.

Hangul is the Korean alphabet. Start with just three vowels. Audio reads 아, 오 and 이: each vowel sits in a block with a silent circle holder. You only need to identify the vowel today; we will study the circle in Lesson 10. Romanization is a temporary sound label, not Korean spelling.

**Example:** 아 · 오 · 이 — a · o · i
ㅏ points right. ㅗ points up. ㅣ is a straight vertical line.
